God shows the most love imaginable to the worst subjects imaginable.

I walk through five steps (or phases) of how God addressed the unfaithfulness of Israel in Hosea chapters 1–2.

1. **Warning.** God warns the people about what is about to happen to them because they've abandoned Him. (Hosea 1:3–9)
2. **Disenchantment.** God takes away their illusions about the other "lovers" they've found. (Hosea 2:5–7)
3. **Separation.** God pulls back all of the good blessings He's been giving them. (Hosea 2:8–13)
4. **Persuades.** God tenderly persuades them to return to Him. (Hosea 2:14–16)
5. **Happily Ever After.** Instead of *no mercy*, **mercy.** Instead of *not mine*, **mine.** Instead of *not yours*, **yours.** And this, *forever*. (Hosea 2:19–23)

Return to the LORD, and He will forgive you. Hosea 14:1–4,9.
